# WebTea HQ

> **Digital work, made simple.**

WebTea HQ is an **AI-powered digital agency and startup** headquartered in **Bettiah, Bihar, India**, founded on **7 July 2026** by **Aryan Singh** and **Ayush Singh**.

We help businesses **establish, build, automate and grow** their digital presence.

## What does “WebTea” mean?

**Web** represents work related to the internet. **Tea** is a familiar beverage.

Together, **WebTea** expresses the idea of making online-related work as simple as sipping tea.

**HQ** means **Headquarters**.

So, **WebTea HQ** is the headquarters for making digital work feel simple.

## What WebTea HQ does

WebTea HQ combines creative execution, technology, marketing and automation rather than treating them as disconnected services.

- Web design and development
- Web apps and digital products
- AI-powered systems and assistants
- Business automation and workflows
- SEO and digital growth
- Social media and marketing
- Content creation and editing
- Shoots and creative production
- Motion graphics and digital advertising
- Brand and digital presence work

The company primarily serves **Indian clients** and can work with **international clients**.

## How we think

1. **Start with the business** — technology is the means; the business problem is the starting point.
2. **Make complexity feel simple** — sophisticated systems should still feel clear to the people using them.
3. **Build systems, not just assets** — websites, content and campaigns become more valuable when they connect to a larger operating system.
4. **AI with human direction** — AI accelerates execution; people set intent, make decisions and protect quality.

## Search and AI discoverability

This repository is a public company knowledge layer for WebTea HQ. It intentionally repeats the same canonical facts in human-readable and machine-readable formats.

- **README.md** — company entity overview
- **src/data/company.mjs** — structured source of truth used by the website
- **index.html** — metadata and Organization JSON-LD
- **public/llms.txt** — compact AI-readable company brief
- **public/robots.txt** — crawler access policy
- **public/sitemap.xml** — sitemap placeholder until the official domain is configured

A GitHub README does **not** guarantee Google or AI search visibility. The strongest entity setup will come from publishing the same verified information on the official website, linking the founders and company profiles consistently, using structured data, maintaining a crawlable sitemap and building genuine external references.
